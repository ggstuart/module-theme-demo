Markdown is a way of writing formatted text using ordinary characters. You can learn the useful parts in five minutes.

<!--more-->

You have probably seen Markdown already without noticing: putting `*stars*` around a word to make it stand out, or starting lines with `-` to make a list.
This unit shows you the handful of rules you need, then you make an exercise and a lecture of your own.
