> From GitHub to your module in three steps.

> Step 1: **Download** `course-package.zip` from GitHub

> Step 2: **Import** it in Learning Zone (*Module Tools > Course Admin > Import/Export/Copy Components*)

> Step 3: **Check** the new folder in your module's Content

> The zip is on the run's page of the **Learning Zone package** workflow, under *Artifacts*.
