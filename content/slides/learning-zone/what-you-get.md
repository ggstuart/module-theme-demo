> One import gives you:

- a **folder** for the module
- a **folder** for each unit
- a **topic** for each lecture and exercise
- in the same order as on the website

Lectures stay slide decks, and exercises keep their contents list and copy buttons.

> Everything is inside the package, so it works without the website.
