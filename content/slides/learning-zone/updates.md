> An import is a copy of your materials at that moment.

> **Learning Zone**: when you change your materials, the **Learning Zone package** workflow builds a fresh zip.
> Import it, then hide or delete the old folder.

> **The website** is always up to date by itself: every commit to `main` rebuilds it within a few minutes.

> Import again whenever you want Learning Zone to catch up.
