> Markdown is plain text with a few symbols that mean *"make this a heading"*, *"make this bold"* or *"this is a list"*.

```markdown {linenos=false}
## Week 1 lab

Bring a **laptop** and your university login.

- open the lab sheet
- work through the steps
- ask if you get stuck
```

You type it in any text editor, or straight into GitHub in your browser.
The website and Learning Zone turn it into nicely formatted pages.
