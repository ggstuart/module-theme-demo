> This is *all* there is to a slide.

```markdown {linenos=false}
---
title: Photosynthesis
---

Plants turn **light** into food.

- needs water
- needs carbon dioxide
- makes oxygen
```

A few lines at the top give it a title.
Below that you just type, using `**stars**` for bold and `-` for bullet points.
No text boxes, no fonts, no slide master.

> Save it in `content/slides/` and add it to a lecture's list of slides.
