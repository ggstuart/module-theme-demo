> **Fast to write**: no fiddling with text boxes or fonts

> **Safe**: every change is saved, and you can always go back

> **Consistent**: every slide and page has the same, accessible style

> **Works everywhere**: phones, tablets, screen readers

> **Easy to update**: fix a typo in one place, everywhere is fixed

> **Code friendly**: coloured code examples with copy buttons

> **Shareable**: colleagues can reuse your slides in their own lectures

> **Yours**: plain text files that will still open in 20 years
