> Write your materials once, as plain text.

> **Slides** for the lecture theatre. A lecture is just a list of slide files, so you can reorder and reuse them.

> **Exercises** for the lab: step-by-step pages with a contents list, and copy buttons on the code.

> **A website** for students: every unit, lecture and exercise, on any device, any time.

> Get them all for free, and put them **in Learning Zone** with one upload.
> No PowerPoint, no Word, no copying and pasting between them.
