- **Arrow keys** or **swipe** to move
- **Full screen** button (or press `f`) in Learning Zone
- Press `m` on the website for the menu

Present straight from Learning Zone or the website, on any computer with a browser.
There is no file to carry around and nothing to install in the lecture theatre.
