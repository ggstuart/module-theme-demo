---
title: Teaching materials with Hugo
---

This small module shows what it is like to write teaching materials as plain text files and publish them both as a **website** and as content in **Learning Zone**.

It is written for lecturers who have never used Markdown or a static website.
Each unit answers one question:

1. **Welcome**: what is this, and what do students see?
2. **Getting started**: how do I get my own copy?
3. **Writing in Markdown**: how hard is the writing?
4. **Lectures and slides**: how do slides work?
5. **Into Learning Zone**: how does it get into my module?

Everything you are looking at, including this page, was written in the same way you will write yours.
