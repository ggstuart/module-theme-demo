The idea

Write your materials once, as plain text. Get slides, exercises and a website for free. Put them in Learning Zone with one upload.

No PowerPoint, no Word, no copying and pasting between them.

1

What you write

---
title: Photosynthesis
---

Plants turn **light** into food.

- needs water
- needs carbon dioxide
- makes oxygen

This is all there is to a slide.

A few lines at the top give it a title. Below that you just type, using **stars** for bold and - for bullet points.

2

What students see

Plants turn light into food.

  • needs water
  • needs carbon dioxide
  • makes oxygen

The same text, styled and laid out for you. Students move through slides with the arrow keys, by swiping on a phone, or with the buttons at the bottom.

4

Why bother?

  • Fast to write: no fiddling with text boxes or fonts
  • Consistent: every slide and page has the same, accessible style
  • Easy to update: fix a typo in one place, everywhere is fixed
  • Shareable: colleagues can reuse your slides in their own lectures
  • Safe: every change is saved, and you can always go back
  • Works everywhere: phones, tablets, screen readers
  • Code friendly: coloured code examples with copy buttons
  • Yours: plain text files that will still open in 20 years
5

So, how hard is it?

If you can write an email, you can write these materials.

You need a free GitHub account and a web browser. Nothing needs installing, and the rest of this module shows you every step.
